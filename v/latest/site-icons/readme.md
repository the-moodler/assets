## About

These icons mark a link that goes to another site. Each one is either a **copy** of the site's official icon or a **recreation** of it, drawn to fit The Moodler's own style. Every site below has a link to its official icon.

The names, logos and trademarks belong to their owners. They're used only to say which site a link goes to. No rights to them are claimed, and their owners don't endorse The Moodler. See the ![file] [`LICENSE`](https://github.com/the-moodler/assets/blob/master/LICENSE).

## The sites

### ![github-24]GitHub

- **Official icon:** [mark-github](https://primer.style/octicons/icon/mark-github-24/) in GitHub's own icon set, Octicons. GitHub's logos are in its [brand guide](https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo).
- **Ours:** a copy. The shape is unchanged and the colour is grey, so it shows on light and dark pages.
- **Files:** [github-16.png](https://the-moodler.github.io/assets/v/latest/site-icons/github-16.png) · [github-16.svg](https://the-moodler.github.io/assets/v/latest/site-icons/github-16.svg) · [github-24.png](https://the-moodler.github.io/assets/v/latest/site-icons/github-24.png) · [github-24.svg](https://the-moodler.github.io/assets/v/latest/site-icons/github-24.svg) · [github-256.png](https://the-moodler.github.io/assets/v/latest/site-icons/github-256.png)
- **Owner:** GitHub, Inc. GitHub and its mark are its trademarks. Octicons are under the MIT licence.

### ![moodle-24]Moodle

- **Official icon:** [moodlelogo.svg](https://github.com/moodle/moodle/blob/main/public/pix/moodlelogo.svg) and [favicon.ico](https://github.com/moodle/moodle/blob/main/public/theme/boost/pix/favicon.ico) in Moodle's own code.
- **Ours:** a recreation. The SVG has the "m" and hat from Moodle's logo, unchanged in shape, in white on a yellow circle. Moodle's own are an orange "m" with a black hat and no circle.
- **Files:** [moodle-16.png](https://the-moodler.github.io/assets/v/latest/site-icons/moodle-16.png) · [moodle-24.png](https://the-moodler.github.io/assets/v/latest/site-icons/moodle-24.png) · [moodle-200.png](https://the-moodler.github.io/assets/v/latest/site-icons/moodle-200.png) · [moodle-256.png](https://the-moodler.github.io/assets/v/latest/site-icons/moodle-256.png) · [moodle-256.svg](https://the-moodler.github.io/assets/v/latest/site-icons/moodle-256.svg)
